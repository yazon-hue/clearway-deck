Seed pitch deck · 2026

01 — The problem

Booked the same way it was 20 years ago

Waste removal and house moves are one of the last hyperlocal trades with no trusted, instant-booking operator.

01

No price certainty

Quotes vary by roughly 18% between local firms, so customers collect three before trusting any of them.

02

No instant booking

Almost every operator quotes over WhatsApp or a phone call — nobody can book a fixed-price slot online today.

03

Two trades, one need

A house move almost always includes a clearance job, but the two trades are rarely offered together.

02 — The solution

One booking platform, fixed price, minutes to confirm

01

Instant quote

Service, load size, postcode and date — a fixed price band in seconds, no callout needed.

02

Book online

Choose a slot and confirm — no back-and-forth over text or waiting on a callback.

03

Tracked arrival

A vetted local team or subcontractor completes the job at the quoted price, every time.

04 — Product

The MVP is deliberately lean

Validate demand and pricing before investing in fleet or software.

Front end

Instant-quote booking page — service toggle, load size, postcode, fixed price band, one-tap booking.

Back end

Manual dispatch at launch — bookings routed by text to a rented van or subcontractor, no software build required yet.

Pricing engine

Fixed price bands by volume and distance, calibrated from real job costs during validation.

Validation gate

10–15 paid jobs before any spend on a vehicle or a dispatch platform.

$

07 — Go-to-market

Hyperlocal first — win one town, then expand

Local SEO

Google Business Profile and one landing page per town — captures "near me" search intent.

Referral partners

Estate agents, letting agents, landlords and skip-hire firms as ongoing referral sources.

Social proof content

Before/after clearance and move footage builds trust faster than ads for an unknown brand.

Paid search — phase 2

Google Ads switched on once cost-per-job and conversion rate are proven from organic channels.
